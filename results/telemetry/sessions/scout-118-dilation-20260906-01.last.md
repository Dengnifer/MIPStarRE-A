### Suggested approach

Let \(E_s=\mathcal H_s\otimes D\), where \(D\) is the Hilbert space indexed by `PauliRegister P`; let \(K\) be the first Naimark ancilla. Write \(J_s:E_s\to E_s\otimes K\) for insertion of its `none` coordinate, and \(\Omega\) for the soundness polynomial outcomes. Compress the soundness POVMs \(G_s\) to \(C_s(g)=J_s^\dagger G_s(g)J_s\). The exact square is:

```text
POVM(Ω, E_s ⊗ K)  -- ground compression -->  POVM(Ω, E_s)
       | f_*                                      | f_*
       v                                          v
POVM(β, E_s ⊗ K)  -- ground compression -->  POVM(β, E_s)
```

All three cross-player consistency bounds survive this compression exactly. Apply the existing rounding theorem to \(C_A,C_B\), obtaining projective \(P_A,P_B\) on \(E_A,E_B\). Put \(r=220\delta^{1/4}\), where \(\delta\) bounds their original polynomial-label inconsistency.

The following **mathematical derivation has not been elaborated in Lean**. The overlap estimate gives \(\operatorname{Cons}(P_A,C_B)\le\delta+\sqrt r\). For any question-dependent outcome map \(f_x\) and opposite-player measurement family \(N_B^x\), data processing and the four-family consistency triangle give
\[
 \operatorname{Cons}_{\mu}(P_A^{f_x},N_B^x)
 \le \delta+\sqrt r+2\sqrt{\delta+\eta},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Cons}_{\mu}(C_A^{f_x},N_B^x)\le\eta.
\]
Instantiate the triangle with \(P_A^{f_x},C_B^{f_x},C_A^{f_x},N_B^x\). Thus polynomial evaluation introduces **no polynomial-outcome cardinality factor**. For \(\eta=\delta\le1\), the bound is \(O(\delta^{1/8})\). Bob follows by explicit factor exchange.

The dependency chain is therefore: established projective game and passing estimate → existing soundness → exact POVM compression → existing same-space rounding plus the displayed transport lemma → source polynomial-image mass argument → projective restriction/completion → unchanged fixed-space witness → existing extraction targets. Report the additional fractional-power term to the scalar work; this scout does not redo #275.

For the literal Naimark architecture, redilate the compressed \(C_s\) on \(E_s\otimes L_s\), then reassociate this as \((\mathcal H_s\otimes L_s)\otimes D\). Define the padded setting \(\widetilde S\) using original measurements tensored with \(I_{L_s}\). This yields a witness for \(\widetilde S\), not for \(S\). Its final local map has domain/codomain
\[
 \mathcal H_s\xrightarrow{j_s}\mathcal H_s\otimes L_s
 \xrightarrow{\text{append EPR}}((\mathcal H_s\otimes L_s)\otimes D)\otimes D
 \xrightarrow{V_s}((\mathcal H_s\otimes L_s)\otimes D)\otimes D.
\]
The existing final witness accommodates the enlarged auxiliary factor. Isometry composition alone does not transfer its operator estimates: the range projection must be retained.
