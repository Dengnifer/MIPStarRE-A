Bind publication to the captured SHA and have the short native-hook invocation verify the advertised tuple, failing closed if either side moved. Add a regression test that moves a ref during preflight.

The helper unconditionally ignores it. Either expose an explicit helper bypass or document and implement a precise protocol exception.
