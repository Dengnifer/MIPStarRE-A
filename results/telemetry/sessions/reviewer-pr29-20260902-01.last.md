Changes are required because several source-linked statements are either false at their current generality or omit material parts of the cited results.
F1–F3 drift from `references/qpbt-paper/06_nonlocal_games_and_mipstar.tex:144-180,232-248`. The paper uses projective measurements or POVMs, normalized states, probability distributions, and the game’s actual strategy data. Raw generalized helpers may remain as Lean-only declarations, but the declarations linked with `\leanok` need paper-facing wrappers.
F4 and F6 fail by scaling. On a singleton question space with opposite deterministic binary measurements and `‖ψ‖ = N`, F4 reduces to `N² ≤ 2N`; F6 admits premises `ε = γ = 0`, `δ = N²` and the same false conclusion. The existing paper-faithful LDT theorem at `MIPStarRE/LDT/Preliminaries/Triangles/SimEq.lean:24` explicitly requires normalization and mass at most one.

F5, F11, and F14 lose the arbitrary finite function collections from paper lines 347–350, 466–478, and 504–516. The sandwich restriction is especially load-bearing: the full function space contains pairs differing at one point, so the required small-collision hypothesis generally fails. F9 and F13 also omit the POVM laws explicitly asserted at paper lines 484–493 and 514–523.
F8 and F10 place implicit type parameters, and in F10 `k`, outside the existential constant. This permits per-instance constants, contrary to the universal quantitative contract and `local/briefs/0016-orchestrator-task.md:83-87`.

The four linked theorems state multiplication, powers, finite order, and twisted commutation only; finite order alone does not imply unitarity for an arbitrary complex matrix.
