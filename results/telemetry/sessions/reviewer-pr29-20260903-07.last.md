## Review
F1: `lem:delta-bound` in `references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex:1520-1528` concerns the nonnegative failure parameter from `thm:pauli` at lines 1426-1447. Lean quantifies over every real `ε`. This is materially false under Mathlib’s negative-base `Real.rpow`: taking `a = 1`, `b = 3/4`, positive and negative large `ε` forces `b' = b` and equality of the leading coefficients. But `R = 4` and `R = 8` both give `m = 8`, while `log₂ R` is respectively 2 and 3, so no universal `a' > 0` satisfies both. Add `0 ≤ ε`; also correct the “same hypotheses” claim in `docs/paper-gaps/qpbt_delta-bound-exponent-comparison.tex:89-94`.
F2: The skeleton brief inventories these holes, so they are known debt. Nevertheless, `docs/PROOF_INTEGRITY.md` classifies direct `sorry` as a merge blocker and relaxes that rule only for a narrow source-realignment PR. This broad addition of new interfaces does not meet that exception. Importing all affected files from `MIPStarRE/QPBT.lean` exposes the incomplete declarations project-wide.
F3: `def:spcc` at `references/qpbt-paper/06_nonlocal_games_and_mipstar.tex:176-180` says that a strategy for a game is PCC. Here `G` supplies only alphabet types: `μ` need not equal `G.μ`, `ψ` need not equal a strategy state, and neither is required to be normalized. Keep this generic relation as `IsPCCOn`, then define the blueprint-linked `Strategy.IsPCC` on `S : Strategy G`, using `G.μ`, `S.ψ`, and the required common-space transport.
F4: At paper lines 144-174, `def:consistent-measurement` starts with a projective measurement on a quantum state, and `def:consistent-strategy` starts with a projective strategy. The Lean predicates retain only the vector equality; for example, every POVM is “consistent” on `ψ = 0`. A raw equality helper is reasonable, but the source-linked predicates must also encode projectivity and normalization.
F5: Paper lines 232-248 and blueprint `ch12_qpbt_games.tex:195-208,239-240` define consistency for probability distributions, bipartite POVMs, unit states, and implicit families satisfying an `O(δ)` bound. `IsConsistentWithin` instead accepts arbitrary operator families, an unnormalized `Distribution`, and any vector, with the fixed inequality `≤ δ`. `AreCloseStrategies` similarly substitutes pointwise unit-constant bounds and omits `δ ∈ [0,1]`. Preserve these concrete metrics as Lean-only helpers, but do not mark them as exact formalizations of the asymptotic source relations.
F6: `def:typed-cl-distributions` at `references/qpbt-paper/07_types.tex:84-93` and blueprint `ch12_qpbt_games.tex:624-629` assumes that both families are typed conditionally linear functions. The adjacent `IsTypedCondLinearFamily` predicate is currently unused. Bundle those proofs or accept the common level and both predicates; a generalized raw bind should have a separate helper name.
F7: `def:line-point-dist` at paper lines 274-287 and blueprint `ch13_qpbt_test.tex:85-96` defines an axis outcome as `(u₀,e_i)` with `i = χ(s)`, thereby forgetting `s`. `LineDesc.axis` stores `s`, so seeds with the same index remain distinct outcomes and the resulting distribution is not the cited pushforward. Project to the index/direction, or document and blueprint an explicitly different refined distribution.
F8: `degPolyEval` is the specialization of `evalCoefficient` already imported from `MIPStarRE/QPBT/Test/LowDegreeGame.lean:265`. Reuse that declaration or move the generic evaluator to the shared polynomial module.
The exact-head CI is reported green and `git diff --check` is clean; those checks do not discharge the proof and source-contract blockers above.
