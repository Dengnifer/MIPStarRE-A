## Review
F1 remains unresolved from round 1. Replacing the duplicate structure with an abbreviation unified the underlying predicate, but the alias is unused in this head and exists solely for another branch. That directly conflicts with `docs/CONTRIBUTING.md:385-386`: “Do not add an empty pass-through abbreviation merely to introduce a second public name.” Delete it and update issue #104 to use `IsBinaryObservable`.
F2 is source-statement drift in the advertised distance definition. At `thm:ms-rigidity`, `references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex:644-646` defines `M ≈_δ N` by
`⟨ψ|(M-N)†(M-N)|ψ⟩ ≤ O(δ)`; the blueprint repeats this at `blueprint/src/chapter/ch13_qpbt_test.tex:249`. `CloseOn ψ δ M N` instead means `‖(M-N)ψ‖ ≤ δ`. Consequently, `msVarObsA_anticommute` and `msVarObsB_anticommute` yield a quadratic defect bounded by `624² ε`, not the same-parameter relation claimed by the docstring. The estimate is potentially stronger after a proper parameter conversion, but the public definition must either be documented as an internal norm-level relation with an explicit conversion to `opDistSq`, or be reparameterized to match the cited notation.
F3: `CloseOn` and its core API are introduced in `Reflections.lean`, but `.smul` appears in `CellRelations.lean:602` and `.neg` appears in `Anticommutation.lean:198`. These generic closure lemmas should live beside the definition so their availability does not depend on importing unrelated later stages.
F4 duplicates the already imported public lemma `MIPStarRE.LDT.MakingMeasurementsProjective.isProj_kronecker` at `MIPStarRE/LDT/MakingMeasurementsProjective/NaimarkCore.lean:59`. Unfolding `heteroKron` and applying that lemma removes the duplicate proof and its unnecessary `DecidableEq` assumptions.
The actual Magic Square calculation is sound: the three constraint steps use the correct incidences, the negative sign comes from the third column, and the accumulated budget is `24 + 120 + 72 + 120 + 72 + 120 + 72 + 24 = 624`. No bridge, residual, or obligation hypotheses were added. The source-facing `exists_ms_rigidity` statement is unchanged and retains the paper’s quantifier order and conclusions.
