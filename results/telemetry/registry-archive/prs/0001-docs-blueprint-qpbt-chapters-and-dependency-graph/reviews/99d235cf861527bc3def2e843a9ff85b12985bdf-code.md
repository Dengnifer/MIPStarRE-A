## Review

Changes are required. Twelve mathematical blockers remain, several inherited from the paper but currently presented as completed definitions or proofs.

F1. `def:graph-distribution` now correctly requires a nonempty edge set, but `def:typed-cl-distributions` accepts an arbitrary graph and immediately invokes `μ_G`. The source has the same implicit boundary omission at `references/qpbt-paper/07_types.tex:65-92`, label `def:graph-distribution`, where the normalization is `1/(2m-k)`. Propagate `E ≠ ∅` to the typed definition and downstream uses.

F2. `lem:ld-sandwich` samples uniformly from `Y` without assuming it finite and nonempty. It also post-processes into arbitrary `R_i`, while `def:bracket` at lines 28-38 requires a finite target. The omission is inherited from `references/qpbt-paper/06_nonlocal_games_and_mipstar.tex:465-495`, label `lem:ld-sandwich`; the cited `references/neexp-paper/05_quantum_preliminaries.tex:952-973`, label `fact:low-degree-sandwich`, instead uses a specified probability distribution. Add the boundary hypotheses or use finite evaluation images.

F3. By `def:povm-distance` at lines 192-199 and its explicit interpretation at lines 210-212, `A≈_δB` supplies only an `O(δ)` bound. The operator inequalities preserve that hidden constant; they do not prove the literal `≤δ` conclusions at lines 253 and 270. The source repeats the imprecision at `references/qpbt-paper/06_nonlocal_games_and_mipstar.tex:347-380`, labels `fact:add-a-proj2` and `lem:cool-closeness-fact`. Replace both final bounds by `O(δ)`.

F4. The paper fixes `N` as the positive integers at `references/qpbt-paper/04_preliminaries.tex:4-7` and takes `m,d,ldc∈N` at `references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex:33-35`, label `sec:ld-game`. At `(q,m,d,k)=(2,1,0,1)`, the blueprint's `δ_ld` is zero for every `ε`; taking `ε=1` admits arbitrary strategies, while deterministic players returning different point values contradict the three exact measurement conclusions. Require `d,k≥1`; any harmless `k=0` extension should be a separate corollary.

F5. `def:ld-win-predicate` replaces the paper's ambiguous zero-direction clause with universal quantification over every valid `t`. That is a defensible correction, but line 146 then falsely says the modified game “coincides” with the external tensor-code test. The source proof at `references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex:442-454`, label `lem:ld-soundness`, depends on that identity. Specify a canonical zero-direction test and prove the required monotonicity or soundness transfer.

F6. The paper introduces `q,m,d∈N` at `references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex:903-905`; its `def:admissible` at lines 958-960 adds field admissibility and `m|q` within that positive domain. The blueprint instead calls `(2,1,0)` admissible. Then `δ_qld=0` even for `ε=1`, so `thm:pauli` would map an arbitrary product strategy exactly to an EPR-containing state, impossible under local isometries. The honest line answers and `def:combine-map` also cease to have degree at most zero. Add `d≥1` and revise the `d=0` discussion without discarding the corrected probability calculation.

F7. The source answer format at `references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex:330-345`, Figure `fig:ld-decider`, consists of coefficient lists for degree-`d` or degree-`md` univariate polynomials. Defining `deg_md(ℓ)` as functions on the geometric set `ℓ` collapses distinct representatives when the degree is large and collapses every zero-direction parameterization to a single constant function. Consequently the original strategy measurement cannot be indexed as in `def:expanded-line-measurement`, and the universal zero-direction check is lost. Retain parameterized polynomial representatives and explicitly embed degree-`d` ALine outcomes.

F8. The exact last inequality in `lem:povm-to-obs` should be `O(|A|δ)`, and line 869 of `lem:qld-comm-line-cons` should be `O(ε)`. Their approximation conclusions survive after retaining hidden constants, but the displayed proofs do not follow from `def:povm-distance`.

F9. The actual diagonal sampler sets `w=π_{j-1}(v)`, so only coordinates before `j=χ(s)` vanish; `w_j` may vanish and `w=0` has positive probability. Thus line 562's “first nonzero coordinate” assertion is false. Lines 565-570 also choose `s_X,s_Z` without the uniform laws on their `χ`-fibres, while Property 2 mixes distributions on line-point pairs as if they were distributions on lines. Compare `references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex:230-271` and the inherited defective proof at `references/qpbt-paper/14_analysis_of_the_pauli_basis_test.tex:1063-1115`, label `lem:qld-sublines`. Taking `ε=m^-4` and admissible `q` sufficiently large makes both advertised arguments `m²ε` and `md/q` tend to zero while `mε^(1/4)=1`.
