## Review

F1 is a proof-correctness blocker. `def:ideg-deg-polynomials` explicitly says at lines 67–78 that evaluation classes on zero-direction lines form only a submeasurement. Nevertheless, line 443 uses `\simeq_\eps`, which `def:consistency` defines only for POVMs, and lines 992–997 invoke a fact requiring two projective measurements. The source makes the same false completeness assumption at `references/qpbt-paper/14_analysis_of_the_pauli_basis_test.tex:627-637`, label `lem:qld-comm-line-cons`. Complete the families with \(A_\perp=I-\sum_aA_a\) and \(B_\perp=0\), including the already-derived missing-mass bound, or prove an explicit submeasurement analogue.

F2 is source-statement drift. The actual Natarajan–Vidick theorem gives exact linearity for every pair but only averaged closeness,
\(\mathbb E_u\,\mathrm{DIS}_{\rho'}(\mathcal A(u),A(u))^2\le\delta\), not closeness for every \(u\) ([Theorem 10, Equation (8)](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.03574#S3)). The QPBT mirror misquotes it at `references/qpbt-paper/14_analysis_of_the_pauli_basis_test.tex:713-724`, label `thm:linearity`; the blueprint must not inherit that strengthening. Restate the closeness on average and update `rem:linearity-import`. `lem:qld-4-10` already uses only the averaged form at lines 212–215.

F3 is ill-posed on any \((x,y_1)\) outside the support of the relevant marginal. The defect is inherited from `references/qpbt-paper/06_nonlocal_games_and_mipstar.tex:504-505`, label `lem:pasting`. Restrict the hypothesis to the marginal support or specify a conditional kernel on null fibers.

F4 contradicts the current proof status. Chapter 16 line 292 says the proof is complete only modulo the unresolved \(2m+2\mid q\) obligation, while the gap note lines 233–239 concludes that the source route does not establish `thm:pauli` for admissible \(m\ge2\). Lines 6 and 394 should describe an open, explicitly tracked proof obligation rather than a completed proof.

F5 correctly identifies that the external tensor-code test samples geometric coordinates directly, but equality of the induced geometric line law is not equality of games. The current raw `ALine` and `DLine` questions retain \(s\in\mathbb F_q\), and a strategy may choose seed-dependent measurements; see `references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex:330-343`. The note must define the direct game and prove averaging/pullback of these measurements, preservation of success, and any required projectivization before claiming that the combining proofs are unchanged. The external test’s direct sampling is confirmed in its [test definition](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.08131#S3).

F6 and F7 violate `docs/paper-gaps/policy.tex:206-212` and lines 248–252 respectively. Populate issue 0002 or create dedicated child issues with the source assertion, obstruction, and repair target; then replace both local-path bibliography notes with published URLs.
