## Review

Changes are required.

F1–F3 are source-statement drift. The paper’s `lem:qld-4-13` states
`\delta_combine = poly(m²ε, md/q)` without divisibility assumptions
(`references/qpbt-paper/14_analysis_of_the_pauli_basis_test.tex:1020-1034`).
The blueprint instead states `m·poly(ε,md/q)` and assumes `2m+2 ∣ q`, while
still claiming `\proves{lem:qld-4-13}`. Likewise, the paper’s
`lem:qld-sublines` at lines 1063–1069 and `lem:qld-4-7` at lines 1267–1274
have no such premise. The blueprint construction genuinely uses
`\chi_{2m+2}`, so this is proof debt, not a boundary condition. Preserve each
paper-labelled statement as unresolved and move the useful restricted results
under explicitly conditional labels, with a policy-compliant paper-gap note.

F4 is the resulting invalid final proof. Chapter 16 assumes only admissibility
but imports the restricted `lem:qld-4-7` and then claims
`\proves{thm:pauli}`. Paper admissibility only requires admissible `q` and
`m ∣ q` (`references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex:958-960`);
`thm:pauli` covers every such tuple at lines 1431–1445. For example,
`(q,m,d)=(8,2,1)` is admissible but `6 ∤ 8`. The unrestricted proof claim must
remain open until the missing construction is supplied.

F5 changes the quantifier structure of `lem:symmetric-strat`. The source
asserts existence at value at least `1-ε`
(`references/qpbt-paper/06_nonlocal_games_and_mipstar.tex:94-99`), whereas the
blueprint proves only the approximation for every `ε'>ε`. The attainment
objection may identify a genuine paper gap, but the corrected theorem needs a
distinct label; the source-labelled declaration must retain the paper statement.

F6’s last “trivial” inequality is reversed when `1-md/q<0`. For
`(q,m,d)=(2,1,3)`, it asserts `-1/2 ≥ -1/4`. The source assertion is
`fact:omega-anticomm-prob` at
`references/qpbt-paper/14_analysis_of_the_pauli_basis_test.tex:70-78`.
The conclusion can be repaired by splitting on `md≤q`; in the other case its
target lower bound is nonpositive.

F7 prevents a faithful formal definition: later uses include arbitrary
matrices, products, commutators, and zero. The intended general definition is
explicit in `references/neexp-paper/05_quantum_preliminaries.tex:385-391`.
Define state-dependent distance for finite operator families and specialize it
to POVMs.

F8 starts induction at level one although `def:cl-func` includes level zero.
When all `ℓ_j=0`, the direct sum is the zero function and needs its own case.
Also state `m≥1`, since `max_j ℓ_j` otherwise has no index.

F9 and F10 repeat malformed source domains
(`references/qpbt-paper/04_preliminaries.tex:435-438` and lines 1172–1199).
Require `k≥1` for the field extension and take `L∈ℕ` for tensor powers. These
are legitimate boundary corrections.

F11 conflicts with both admissibility and the chapter-11 warning that
evaluation is noninjective for `d≥q`; `(8,1,8)` is already a counterexample.
Retain polynomial-representative semantics and split off any trivial
large-degree regime where evaluation injectivity is needed.
