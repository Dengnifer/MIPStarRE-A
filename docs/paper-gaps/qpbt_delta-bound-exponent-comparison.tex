\section{Key theorem forms}

Let $a\geq 1$ and $0<b<1$ be the universal constants in the soundness
function
\[
  \delta_{\mathrm{qld}}(\eps,m,d,q)
    = a(md)^a\bigl(\eps^b+q^{-b}+2^{-bmd}\bigr).
\]
For $R\geq4$, let $c$ be the least even integer at least $(a+b)/b$, set
$k=c\lceil\log_2\log_2 R\rceil+1$ and $q=2^k$, and let $j$ be determined by
$2^j\leq c\lceil\log_2 R\rceil+1<2^{j+1}$. The remaining canonical parameters
are $m=2^j$ and $d=1$. In particular,
$m\geq (c/2)\log_2 R\geq\log_2 R$. The required estimate for the third summand is
\[
  a m^a 2^{-bm} \leq C'(\log R)^{-b},
\]
for a constant $C'$ depending only on $a$ and $b$.  This estimate, together
with the source bounds for the first two summands, gives the asserted
polylogarithmic bound for $\delta_{\mathrm{qld}}$.

\section{The source comparison}

The canonical parameters are defined in \cite[lines~1503--1514]{Ji2020MIPStar},
and the bound and its proof occupy lines~1520--1562. At line~1543 the proof
replaces $2^{-bmd}$ by $q^{-b}$ using the assertion $k\leq m$.%
\footnote{Tracked in
\href{https://github.com/Dengnifer/MIPStarRE-A/issues/16}{GitHub issue \#16}.
The canonical parameter definition is at
\path{references/qpbt-paper/08_classical_and_quantum_low_degree_tests.tex},
lines~1503--1514; the bound and its proof are at lines~1520--1562, with the
disputed comparison at line~1543.}
That comparison does not follow from the stated parameter choice.  For example,
the allowed values $a=1$ and $b=1/2$ give $c=4$, and $R=5$ gives $k=9$ but
$m=8$.  Thus the displayed justification is false even though the lemma's
conclusion remains valid.

The third term can instead be bounded independently of $q$.  Since $d=1$ and
$m\geq (c/2)\log R\geq\log R$,
\[
  2^{-bm}\leq R^{-b}.
\]
The accompanying upper bound $m\leq 2c\log R$ therefore gives
\[
  a m^a2^{-bm}
    \leq a(2c)^a(\log R)^aR^{-b}
    = a(2c)^a\bigl((\log R)^{a+b}R^{-b}\bigr)(\log R)^{-b}.
\]
The parenthesized factor is bounded on $R\geq4$: it is continuous and tends
to zero as $R$ tends to infinity.  Absorbing its supremum into the universal
constant yields the required estimate.

\section{Conclusion}

The source's comparison $k\leq m$ is false for some permitted constants and
values of $R$.  It is not needed: the canonical lower bound on $m$ controls the
third soundness term directly and proves the same final estimate after enlarging
the universal constant.
