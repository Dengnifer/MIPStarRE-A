\section{At a glance}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Difficulty.} Medium.  The source applies a decoder--encoder
  identity to every bounded individual-degree polynomial, although the identity
  is guaranteed for encoded polynomials and can fail outside the encoding image.
  \item \textbf{Estimated weight.} The restricted algebraic identity and the
  probabilistic non-encoding-mass estimate required algebraic and
  project-specific consistency arguments; both are now proved. No work
  remains on this support estimate. The separate source-facing consistency
  construction is also complete.
  \item \textbf{Mathlib/project split.} Evaluation of the multilinear
  interpolant is elementary polynomial algebra.  The non-encoding-mass bound
  uses project-specific consistency estimates and Schwartz--Zippel.
  \item \textbf{Key mathematical inputs.} Multilinear interpolation on the
  Boolean cube, injectivity of the low-degree encoding, measurement
  consistency, and Schwartz--Zippel.  The correction is tracked in
  \ghissue{19}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Key theorem forms}

For a word $h\colon\{0,1\}^m\to\mathbb F_q$, let $g_h$ be its multilinear
extension.  Define the chapter's decoder by
\[
  \operatorname{Dec}(g)(y)=g(y),
  \qquad y\in\{0,1\}^m.
\]
Then
\[
  \operatorname{Dec}(g_h)=h
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \operatorname{Dec}(g_h)\mathbin{\cdot}\operatorname{ind}_m(u)=g_h(u).
\]
More generally, the second identity holds for a polynomial outcome $g$ under
the explicit hypothesis that $g$ belongs to the image of the encoding; no
converse is asserted.

For an arbitrary bounded individual-degree polynomial $g$, the Boolean values
determine its multilinear interpolant, not necessarily $g$ itself.  The
unrestricted identity
\[
  \operatorname{Dec}(g)\mathbin{\cdot}\operatorname{ind}_m(u)=g(u)
\]
is therefore false.

\section{Counterexample and source comparison}

The low-degree encoding is defined at lines~873--897 of
\path{references/qpbt-paper/04_preliminaries.tex}.  The general decoder at
lines~917--924 retains evaluations lying in a chosen subset $H$; the extraction
chapter instead specifies at lines~1419--1420 of
\path{references/qpbt-paper/14_analysis_of_the_pauli_basis_test.tex} that the
decoder returns all evaluations on the Boolean cube.  This is the specialization
$H=\mathbb F_q$, rather than the Boolean-valued shorthand used earlier
\cite[lines~873--924 and 1419--1420]{Ji2020MIPStar}.

Take $m=1$, $d\geq2$, and a field of characteristic two with more than two
elements.  For $g(x)=x^2$, the Boolean evaluations are $g(0)=0$ and $g(1)=1$.
Consequently the decoder is the word whose multilinear extension is $x$.
For any $u\notin\{0,1\}$ with $u^2\neq u$,
\[
  \operatorname{Dec}(g)\mathbin{\cdot}\operatorname{ind}_1(u)=u
  \neq u^2=g(u).
\]
Thus the equality asserted for every $g$ at lines~1483--1492 of the chapter-14
source mirror is false \cite[lines~1483--1492]{Ji2020MIPStar}.  If degrees at
least $q$ are allowed, equality as a function would not by itself prove encoding
membership either, since $x^q$ and $x$ induce the same function on $\mathbb F_q$.
In particular, $(q,m,d)=(8,1,2)$ is an admissible tuple exhibiting the
failure: $1$ divides $8$, the extension degree is odd, and $d\geq1$.
The polynomial reindexing used at lines~1805--1822 also fails here.  Set
$h=0$ and $h'=h+\operatorname{Dec}(g)$.  Then $g_{h'}=x$, whereas the
printed assertion $g_{h'}=g_h+g$ gives $x^2$.  These are unequal polynomial
representatives, independently of the choice of evaluation point.

\section{Required repair}

The extraction proof must split the marginal measurement into encoding and
non-encoding outcomes.  On encoding outcomes, the restricted identity above
justifies the substitution.  On the complement, the proof must bound
\[
  \sum_{g\ \mathrm{not\ an\ encoding}}
  \langle\widehat\psi,\widehat S_g^W\widehat\psi\rangle.
\]
For a supplied global polynomial-pair witness with point-consistency error
$\delta_G$, the formal proof derives an
$O(\delta_G+\sqrt\eps)+md/q$ bound. Measure the original Pauli answer $h$ and
the ancillary ideal Pauli answer $h'$ and return $g_{h+h'}$. This reference
measurement is supported on encodings. Perfect ancillary consistency and the
Pauli-basis check bound its evaluated inconsistency with the marginal.
A non-encoding polynomial differs from every encoded polynomial, so
Schwartz--Zippel bounds the probability that their evaluations coincide.
Completeness and positivity bound the non-encoding mass by the full polynomial
inconsistency. This uniform estimate holds for both player sides; it is not
inserted as an extraction hypothesis.

The printed reindexing at lines~1805--1822 replaces
$g_{\operatorname{Dec}(g_W)}$ by $g_W$, which is the invalid reverse identity
for an arbitrary polynomial outcome.  In the corrected calculation, the
constraint is
$g_h(u)=\operatorname{Dec}(g_W)\mathbin{\cdot}\operatorname{ind}_m(u)-a$.
After setting $h'=h+\operatorname{Dec}(g_W)$, the defining evaluation formula
$g_k(u)=k\mathbin{\cdot}\operatorname{ind}_m(u)$ and characteristic two turn
that constraint into $g_{h'}(u)=a$.  Thus this step uses neither decoder
linearity nor the identity $\operatorname{Dec}(g_h)=h$
\cite[lines~1805--1822]{Ji2020MIPStar}.

\section{Retention and sufficiency of the correction}

The two printed identities are retained separately as unasserted propositions.
One quantifies over every bounded polynomial $g$ and every field point $u$ and
states $\operatorname{Dec}(g)\cdot\operatorname{ind}_m(u)=g(u)$.
The other quantifies over every bounded polynomial $g$ and every word $h$ and
states $g_{h+\operatorname{Dec}(g)}=g_h+g$ as an equality of polynomial
representatives.  Neither includes an encoding hypothesis.  They must not be
identified: the example $g(x)=x^q$ satisfies the first identity at every point
but violates the second at $h=0$.\footnote{The definitions are
\leanid{MIPStarRE.QPBT.PrintedDecoderEvaluationClaim} and
\leanid{MIPStarRE.QPBT.PrintedDecoderReindexingClaim} in
\path{MIPStarRE/QPBT/Extraction/PrintedClaims.lean}, tracked in
\ghissue{667}.  They have no proofs and are not inputs to any consumer.}

The consumer comparison distinguishes three calculations in the source.
For point consistency at lines~1483--1492, both player orientations separate
encoding outcomes from their complement.  The restricted evaluation identity
applies only in the first sum, and the proved non-encoding estimate bounds the
second.  The source-facing construction supplies one global measurement and
applies both comparisons to it.  The separately stated uniform support estimate
still takes a global measurement as a premise; its proof is not an
unconditional construction and is not used by that composition.

For observable self-consistency at lines~1495--1605, the difference polynomial
$g-g_h$ has decoder $\operatorname{Dec}(g)-h$.  Consequently equality of two
such polynomials gives equality of their decoded labels.  This uses only
linearity and the left-inverse law $\operatorname{Dec}(g_h)=h$, both valid for
the full-field decoder, and requires no encoding condition on $g$.
The corresponding difference-polynomial and observable estimates are proved
separately from the non-encoding estimate.\footnote{The algebraic identity and
its use are \path{decodeFq_pullingPoly},
\path{pulling_label_eq_of_poly_eq}, and
\path{tildeObs_selfConsistent_ofGlobalPairWitness}.  The inverse law is also
used by the encoding-supported reference and by the overlap transfer; these
uses apply it only to an encoded word.}

Finally, the swap-conjugation calculation at lines~1805--1822 uses the exact
cancellation already described, not either retained false proposition.
The ensuing unitary extraction and both final soundness conclusions therefore
receive the same operator identities.  This covers the uses in the blueprint
consistency, swap-conjugation, unitary-extraction, and soundness nodes as well
as the two direct substitutions in the paper.

The repair does not change the decoder specified at lines~1419--1420 or discard
any polynomial outcomes.  The encoding restriction is a hypothesis of the
local identity only, not of the extraction theorem.  No condition $d<q$ is
added: when the collision estimate $md/q$ is large the support bound may be
uninformative, but it remains valid.  Nor is encoding membership claimed to be
necessary for pointwise equality at high degree.

The additional consistency terms do not weaken the source error family.  Write
$\delta_G=a(md)^a(\eps^b+q^{-b}+2^{-bmd})$ for the global-measurement error.
For $0\leq\eps\leq1$, $md\geq1$, and $0<b\leq1/2$, one has
$\sqrt\eps\leq\eps^b$ and $md/q\leq(md)^a q^{-b}$.
Thus $C(\delta_G+\sqrt\eps+md/q)$ is absorbed by choosing the universal
constants before the parameters and strategy, exactly as the source absorbs
these terms after its pulling calculation.  The fourth-root extraction rate
and the final soundness error family are unchanged, including at zero
error.\footnote{The existing declarations \path{deltaExtract_le_deltaQld} and
\path{exists_extractionWitness_fourth_root_rate} perform the downstream
absorption; \path{exists_globalPairWitness} constructs the measurement.
The source-law and tensor-code questions remain separate in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/qpbt_ld-dimension-divisibility.tex}.}
This is a restriction of an invalid intermediate substitution, with its omitted
contribution estimated, not a restriction of the source conclusion.  Independent
hard review
\href{https://github.com/Dengnifer/MIPStarRE-QPBT/pull/672#pullrequestreview-5270109079}%
{5270109079} verified all four adoption conditions, and main adopted this
correction in
\path{results/telemetry/source-adoptions-672-20260922.md}.  The retained
propositions themselves remain unasserted and supply no proof evidence.

\section{Conclusion}

The decoding identity is false for a general polynomial outcome.  Restricting
it to the encoding image is a necessary local correction.  The two specialized
non-encoding-mass estimates required for the pulled-apart point-consistency
conclusion are proved and used in the source-facing construction. The uniform
auxiliary estimate is also proved for a supplied global witness, but the
source-facing construction does not depend on it.
