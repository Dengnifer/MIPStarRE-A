# QPBT paper-gap register

This register records every paper-gap note currently known for the quantum
Pauli basis test track. Where a source statement is itself wrong, the blueprint
entry states the corrected mathematics and cites the note. Where the statement
is not known to be false but its printed proof is blocked, the proof-gap
protocol keeps the source-labelled statement as printed and carries the
correction in the proof, in a chapter remark, and -- where the argument does
prove a weaker form -- in a separate formalization-support node.

The `Terminal status` column applies C3 of the development protocol
`local/protocols/completion.md` (not included in the artifact), independently of the
historical descriptions in the correction column. `open` identifies remaining
mathematical constructions or source assertions; such an assertion may be
unasserted as a `Prop`, or have no Lean declaration at all, despite zero term-level
proof holes. `pending` means that an existing, proved correction has not received
the complete adoption certification described below. Neither value is terminal.
`no-difference` concerns the source conclusion, not identity of proof methods.
